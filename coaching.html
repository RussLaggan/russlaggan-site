What people are asking.

Why doesn't my sphere call me?

They don't trust you as their expert in real estate. They trust someone else more. A good sphere closes at seven or eight percent right now. Under five percent means the people who already know you do not see you as the one to call. Coaching builds your credibility, personal and professional, so those same people see you as their expert and choose you every time.

What do I do when my agents wait for me to bring them the deals?

Your agents are waiting on you because they have no business of their own. First we help each of them get the people who already know them, like them, and trust them to do business with them. Then we add leads, and we build trust with those leads so they see your agent as their expert and do business with them too.

How do I do attraction while doing production?

In the process of doing production, you bump into a ton of real estate agents. Learn how to leverage those agents and add value to them, so they see you as an expert they can trust and are willing to follow. They come to you, instead of you having to chase them.

Does this fit if I run a brokerage?

Yes. Absolutely. If you are running a brokerage and you are struggling with growth, and with getting your agents productive, the coaching, teaching, and training I do helps you build authority and followability. It also gets your agents into production, instead of looking at you as their source for sales.
