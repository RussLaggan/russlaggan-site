Trainer

How would you like Russ to train your team?

Get them on the same page, marching to the same music, and driving the business, versus you driving all the leads. In the room or virtual. Six or eight sessions, Jumpstart style, on a sphere of influence business.

The gap

Most teams are doing next to nothing from the sphere.

Ask a team how much of their business comes from people they already know. The usual answer is about five percent.

The math

Ten people. About two hundred each.

That is about two thousand people in the sphere. Five percent of that is about one hundred closings the team should already be able to produce.

The work

The activities have to be required.

Put everyone they know in a bucket. Work that list. Hold the team to the same metrics. If that minimum is not happening, there is a lot of work to do.
